SUMMARY
================================================================================

AVX-512 Speedup over AVX2 (by Thread Count):
  -  1 thread:  2.02x
  -  2 threads: 2.00x
  -  4 threads: 2.00x
  -  8 threads: 1.97x
  - 16 threads: 1.74x

Maximum Speedups over Baseline:
  - AVX2 (16 threads):   ~33-35x
  - AVX-512 (16 threads): ~56-62x

The AVX-512 implementation achieves consistent ~2x speedup over AVX2 for 
1-8 threads across all input sizes. This matches the theoretical expectation
from doubling the SIMD width (512-bit vs 256-bit vectors).

At 16 threads, speedup decreases to ~1.7x due to memory bandwidth saturation
and thread contention on the 8-core CPU (16 threads = 2x hyperthreading).
